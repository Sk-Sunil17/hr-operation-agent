# Code of Conduct

## 1. What we expect
Everyone at Acme Labs is expected to treat colleagues, candidates, customers and suppliers with respect. Harassment, discrimination, bullying and retaliation are never acceptable and are treated as serious misconduct.

## 2. Raising a concern
A concern may be raised with a manager, with any member of the people team, or directly with the Head of People. A concern may be raised in confidence, and may be raised about a manager without going through that manager.

## 3. How a concern is handled
The people team acknowledges a concern within one working day and tells the person who raised it who is handling it. A named person owns the matter from that point. Concerns are not investigated by automated systems and are never handled by the people assistant.

## 4. Protection from retaliation
Nobody who raises a concern honestly will be disadvantaged for doing so, whatever the outcome of the matter. Retaliation is itself treated as serious misconduct.

## 5. Confidentiality
Information about a concern is shared only with those who need it to handle or decide the matter.
