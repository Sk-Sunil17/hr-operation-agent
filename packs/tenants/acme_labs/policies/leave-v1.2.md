# Leave Policy

## 1. Scope
This policy covers paid annual leave, sick leave, compassionate leave and unpaid leave for everyone employed by Acme Labs. Contractors are covered by their own agreement and this policy does not apply to them.

## 2. Annual leave allowance
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave each calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Part-time employees receive the same allowance in proportion to their contracted hours, rounded up to the nearest half day.

## 3. Requesting annual leave
Leave is requested through the people system, giving at least ten working days' notice before it starts. Requests of more than ten consecutive working days need the agreement of the employee's manager, with at least one month's notice. A manager may decline a request where the team cannot cover the period, and must give the reason in writing.

## 4. Carrying leave over
Up to five unused days may be carried into the next calendar year and must be taken by 31 March. Days beyond five are not carried over and are not paid in lieu, except where leave could not be taken because of a period of sickness.

## 5. Sick leave
Sick leave is paid in full for up to 20 working days in any twelve-month period. An employee should tell their manager on the first day of absence. Absence of more than five consecutive working days requires a medical certificate, which is sent to the people team and not to the manager.

## 6. Compassionate leave
Five days of paid compassionate leave are available on the death or serious illness of a close family member. Further unpaid leave may be agreed with the Head of People.

## 7. Unpaid leave
Unpaid leave of up to three months may be requested in writing. It is granted at the discretion of the Head of People, and continuous service is preserved while it is taken.
