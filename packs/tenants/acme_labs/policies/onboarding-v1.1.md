# New Starter Requirements

## 1. Before the first day
A new starter returns a signed contract, confirms their contact details and emergency contact, provides the documents needed to verify their right to work, and supplies bank details for payroll. These are returned at least five working days before the start date.

## 2. Right to work
The documents needed to verify right to work are checked by the people team before the first day. A start date cannot be confirmed until that check is complete.

## 3. First day
A new starter receives a laptop, their accounts, and a first-week plan from their manager. The people team confirms the record is complete in the people system.

## 4. Probation
The probation period is six months. A manager holds a review at three months and again at five months, and records the outcome in the people system.

## 5. Chasing outstanding items
Where something is outstanding the people team reminds the new starter and their manager. A reminder is sent at most twice; after that the people team raises it with the manager directly rather than sending further reminders.
