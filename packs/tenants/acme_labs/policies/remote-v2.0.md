# Remote and Flexible Working Policy

## 1. Principle
Acme Labs is remote-friendly. Where a role can be done well from home, an employee may work remotely without needing permission for each occasion.

## 2. Office days
Every employee is expected in the office on Tuesdays and Wednesdays. Teams may agree additional shared days among themselves. A manager may ask for attendance on other days for a specific reason such as onboarding a new starter, and should give at least five working days' notice.

## 3. Working outside the usual pattern
An employee who wishes to change their regular working pattern, including compressed hours or a permanent reduction in days, makes a written request to their manager. The manager replies within 20 working days and records the outcome in the people system.

## 4. Working from another country
Working from a country other than the one in the employment contract needs written agreement from the Head of People before travel, and is limited to 20 working days in any twelve-month period.

## 5. Equipment
Acme Labs provides a laptop and one monitor. A contribution of up to 250 towards a desk or chair is available once every three years, claimed through the usual expenses route.
