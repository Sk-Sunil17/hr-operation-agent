# Standards of Conduct

## 1. What is expected
Everyone at Harbour Clinic treats colleagues, patients and visitors with respect. Harassment, discrimination, bullying and retaliation are treated as gross misconduct.

## 2. Raising something
A concern may be raised with any charge nurse, with the People Officer, or with the union representative. It may be raised in confidence, and it may be raised about a line manager without going through them.

## 3. How it is handled
The People Officer acknowledges a concern within one shift and names the person handling it. Concerns are never assessed, summarised or answered by an automated system.

## 4. Union representation
A member of staff covered by the collective agreement may be accompanied by their representative at any meeting held under this policy.

## 5. Protection
Nobody who raises a concern honestly will be disadvantaged for it, whatever the outcome.
