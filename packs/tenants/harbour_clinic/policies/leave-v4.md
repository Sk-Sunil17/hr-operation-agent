# Leave and Time Off

## 1. Who this covers
This policy covers everyone engaged by Harbour Clinic on a permanent or fixed-term basis. Bank and agency staff are engaged under separate terms and this policy does not apply to them.

## 2. Annual leave
Staff accrue annual leave at the rate of 1.75 days for each completed month of service. Part-time staff accrue in proportion to contracted hours. Leave is expressed in hours rather than days for anyone working rotating shifts.

## 3. Requesting leave
Leave is requested through the rota at least 28 days before the first day away, because cover must be arranged before a request can be approved. Requests inside 28 days are considered only where cover already exists.

## 4. Leave at short notice
Where leave is needed at short notice for an urgent personal reason, the charge nurse may approve up to three shifts without the usual notice. Anything longer needs the Practice Manager.

## 5. Sickness
A member of staff who cannot attend a shift tells the charge nurse at least four hours before it starts wherever possible. Absence of more than three consecutive shifts requires a note from a doctor, which goes to the People Officer and not to the charge nurse.

## 6. Carrying leave over
Up to 40 hours of untaken leave may be carried into the next leave year. Anything beyond 40 hours is lost unless it could not be taken because the clinic refused cover.
