# Starting at Harbour Clinic

## 1. Before the first shift
A new member of clinical staff provides a verified professional registration, two clinical references, documents confirming the right to work, bank details for payroll, and a contact number for the rota. These are needed at least fifteen days before the first shift.

## 2. Registration
Professional registration is verified directly with the issuing body and re-verified every twelve months. A first shift cannot be rostered until verification is complete.

## 3. References
Two clinical references are required. A first shift cannot be rostered on one reference.

## 4. First shift
A new member of staff is supernumerary for their first two shifts and is paired with a named colleague.
