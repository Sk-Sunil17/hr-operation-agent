# Code of Conduct

## 1. Standards
Meridian Group requires all employees to act with integrity and to treat colleagues with dignity. Harassment, discrimination, victimisation and bullying constitute serious misconduct.

## 2. Reporting
A matter may be reported to a line manager, to HR Operations, or directly to an Employee Relations Specialist. Reports may be made confidentially.

## 3. Handling
Employee Relations acknowledges a report within one working day and assigns a named owner. Automated systems do not assess, investigate, summarise or respond to reports under this policy.

## 4. Representation
Where the employee is covered by a collective agreement, the employee may be accompanied by a representative at any meeting held under this policy.

## 5. No retaliation
Retaliation against a person who has reported a matter in good faith is itself serious misconduct.
