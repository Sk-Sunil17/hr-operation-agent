# Flexible Working

## 1. Eligibility
An employee with six months' continuous service may request a change to their working pattern, including hours, days or location.

## 2. Making a request
A request is made in writing to the line manager and copied to HR Operations, stating the change sought and the proposed start date.

## 3. Decision
The line manager responds within twenty working days, in consultation with the HR Business Partner. Where a request is declined the business reason is given in writing.

## 4. Office attendance
Employees attend their assigned office at least three days each week unless a flexible working arrangement records otherwise.
