# Flexible Working

## 1. Eligibility
An employee may request a change to their working pattern from their first day of employment, including hours, days or location.

## 2. Making a request
A request is made in the HR service desk and is visible to the line manager and HR Operations.

## 3. Decision
The line manager responds within fifteen working days, in consultation with the HR Business Partner.

## 4. Office attendance
Employees attend their assigned office at least two days each week unless a flexible working arrangement records otherwise.
