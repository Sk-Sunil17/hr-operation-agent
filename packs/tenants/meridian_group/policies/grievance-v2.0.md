# Grievance Procedure

## 1. Purpose
This procedure sets out how an employee raises a concern about their employment and how Meridian Group responds.

## 2. Informal stage
Where appropriate, an employee is encouraged to raise the matter with their line manager first. This stage is optional and may be skipped where the concern is about the line manager.

## 3. Formal stage
A formal grievance is submitted in writing to HR Operations. An Employee Relations Specialist is assigned as owner within one working day and contacts the employee directly.

## 4. Meeting
A grievance meeting is held within ten working days of receipt. The employee may be accompanied.

## 5. Outcome and appeal
The outcome is given in writing within five working days of the meeting. An employee who is dissatisfied may appeal in writing to the Regional Director within ten working days.
