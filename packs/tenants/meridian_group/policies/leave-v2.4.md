# Group Leave Policy

## 1. Application
This policy applies to all employees of Meridian Group.

## 2. Annual leave
Annual leave is granted at 18 days per completed year of service, rising to 21 days after five completed years.

## 3. Approval
Annual leave requires the approval of the line manager.

## 4. Sick leave
Paid sick leave is granted for up to 10 working days per calendar year.
