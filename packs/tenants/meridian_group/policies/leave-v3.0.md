# Group Leave Policy

## 1. Application
This policy applies to all employees of Meridian Group and its entities. Where an entity operates under a collective agreement, that agreement prevails over this policy to the extent of any inconsistency, and the People function records the difference.

## 2. Annual leave
Annual leave is granted at 21 days per completed year of service, rising to 25 days after five completed years. Part-time employees accrue in proportion to contracted hours.

## 3. Approval
Annual leave requires the approval of the line manager and is recorded in the core HR system before it is taken. Requests exceeding fifteen consecutive working days additionally require Regional Director approval.

## 4. Sick leave
Paid sick leave is granted for up to 14 working days per calendar year. A medical certificate is required for any absence exceeding two consecutive working days and is submitted to HR Operations.

## 5. Unused leave
A maximum of seven days may be carried forward with written approval from the HR Business Partner, and must be taken within the first quarter of the following year.

## 6. Leave during notice
Leave taken during a notice period requires approval from the HR Business Partner. Untaken accrued leave at the end of employment is dealt with under the employee's individual contract.
