# New Joiner Requirements (Hong Kong)

## 1. Documents
A new joiner submits a signed letter of appointment, identification for right-to-work verification, bank details for payroll, and emergency contact details, no later than ten working days before the start date.

## 2. Verification
HR Operations completes right-to-work verification before the first day. A start date is not confirmed until verification is complete.

## 3. First week
The line manager provides a first-week plan. Equipment is issued by the local office administrator rather than through the service desk.

## 4. Probation
Probation is six months, with documented reviews at six weeks and four months.
