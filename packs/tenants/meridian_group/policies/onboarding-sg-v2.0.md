# New Joiner Requirements (Singapore)

## 1. Documents
A new joiner submits a signed letter of appointment, identification for right-to-work verification, bank details for payroll, and emergency contact details, no later than seven working days before the start date.

## 2. Verification
HR Operations completes right-to-work verification before the first day. A start date is not confirmed until verification is complete.

## 3. First week
The line manager provides a first-week plan and confirms equipment and system access requests through the HR service desk.

## 4. Probation
Probation is three months, with a documented review at six weeks.
